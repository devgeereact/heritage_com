## Sustainable publishing

Aim for two pieces of content each week, adapted across platforms, not ten separate posts. Record a few clips while doing real work; write captions in one short weekly session.

| Week | Main story | Companion | Useful action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduce the owner and confirmed services | How an appointment works | Request appointment |
| 2 | A genuine completed men's garment | Fabric/fit explanation | Discuss a similar piece |
| 3 | Process detail: cutting or finishing | What to bring to measurements | Send garment brief |
| 4 | Company/coordinated enquiry process | Consented feedback or care tip | Discuss quantity/date |

Instagram/Facebook: project photos and short clips. TikTok: the same useful process footage with an honest short explanation. LinkedIn: one practical company-order/craft/process story every other week. WhatsApp Community: one useful update weekly or less. Adjust to actual time available.

Maintain only a short content list: topic, source image/video, draft caption, consent checked, planned date and published URLs. Existing marketing/CMS records can support drafts if useful. Automatic scheduling and social APIs are optional later.

## Conversion and moderation

All profiles lead to one working enquiry/booking destination. Use simple source tags when useful and avoid putting client references in analytics URLs. Track enquiry source, relevant enquiries, appointments and accepted jobs, not follower growth alone.

Reply within an owner-approved realistic window. Do not promise instant replies or 24/7 support. Move order details to private channels. Remove spam, impersonation and exposed personal records promptly; do not argue publicly about private complaints. Record the resolution on the job privately.

## Setup acceptance

Verify each existing account, ownership and handle before edits. Preview bio, contact details and cover on phone. Test website/contact links, and confirm CMS footer uses the same verified URLs. Enable platform-supported account recovery/MFA. Publish only after approval of facts, asset rights and final text. No social action was taken during this planning task.
