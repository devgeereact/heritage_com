About Herit Clothing
Herit Clothing makes fashion that celebrates African heritage. We take traditional African fabrics, prints and patterns and fuse them with contemporary style, so the modern African can wear their culture with pride. We sell online and from our home in Ajuwon Market, Lagos, to customers near and far. HeritOS is the system that runs the brand behind the scenes, so every order, every metre of fabric and every customer is tracked in one place instead of across notebooks, chats and spreadsheets.
Our Mission
Herit Clothing exists to celebrate and promote African heritage through clothing, fusing traditional fabrics and prints with contemporary style for the modern African identity.
Our Vision
A world where African heritage is worn with pride everywhere, and a brand from Lagos can dress customers across the globe without losing its roots.
What We Build
HeritOS is the brand operating system: products and variants, orders across web and market, stock, customers, marketing and money, with a 2026 AI layer for forecasting and content.
